# Kokumi

Some peptides have no taste of their own but make the food around them taste
fuller. Glutathione and other γ-glutamyl peptides do it through the
calcium-sensing receptor (CaSR) on taste cells, and blocking the receptor takes
the effect away.[^kuroda]

## Levers

- **[Glutathione](../agents/glutathione.md):** garlic, onion, and meat carry
    it.[^ahmad]
- **Heat:** glutathione heated with glucose above 120ºC (248ºF) forms Maillard
    products that activate CaSR more strongly, while glutathione heated alone
    breaks down and loses activity. So far this rests on receptor assays, with
    no tasting panel.[^ahmad]

## Methods

- [Roasting](../methods/dry-heat-cooking.md#roasting) -- dry heat reaches the
    temperature where glutathione's Maillard products form.

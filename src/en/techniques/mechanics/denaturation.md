# Denaturation

Proteins unfold under heat, acid, or salt and bond to their neighbors.[^soc] In
meat, the final temperature strongly affects how tender it gets and how much
weight it loses.[^laakkonen]

## Levers

- **Temperature:** beef heated slowly barely changes in tenderness for 4 hours,
    then softens sharply as it warms from 50ºC (122ºF) to 60ºC (140ºF), while
    its weight loss climbs steadily.[^laakkonen] In veal, the softening comes
    from changes in connective tissue.[^bouton]
- **Acid:** the pH of egg white changes the temperature at which it
    coagulates.[^croguennec]
- **Salt:** in salt solutions like those used in meat processing, muscle
    myofibrils swell to about twice their volume, and water losses on cooking
    may come from the same filaments shrinking.[^offer]
- **Time:** collagen in a tough cut needs 2-3 hours at 85-90ºC (185-195ºF) to
    dissolve into gelatin, depending on the size of the cut.[^blais]

## Methods

- [Poaching](../methods/moist-heat-cooking.md#poaching) -- water below a boil
    sets proteins gently.
- [Acid in poaching liquid](../methods/moist-heat-cooking.md#acid-in-poaching-liquid)
    -- vinegar changes how egg white sets.
- [Braising](../methods/combination-cooking.md#braising) -- collagen dissolves
    into gelatin.
- [Wet-brining](../methods/curing-and-brining.md#wet-brining) -- salted muscle
    proteins hold more water through cooking.
- [Resting](../methods/dry-heat-cooking.md#resting) -- proteins keep setting
    after the heat is off.
- [Whipping egg whites](../methods/mixing-methods.md#whipping-egg-whites) -- egg
    white proteins unfold around air bubbles.

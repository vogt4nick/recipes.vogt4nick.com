# Diffusion

Water and what dissolves in it move from where they are concentrated toward
where they are not. Salt on food draws water out by osmosis,[^rothman] and given
time, the salty juice is drawn back in.[^earl]

## Levers

- **Concentration:** a brine left too long makes meat too salty.[^bomze] Tested
    pickle recipes call for vinegar of 5% acidity in the stated amount, because
    the acid has to reach a safe level.[^uga]
- **Distance:** red wine penetrates less than 1 mm into beef short ribs in 18
    hours.[^atk-marinade]
- **Temperature:** brown rice hydrates twice as fast at 50ºC (122ºF) as at 25ºC
    (77ºF).[^han-lim]
- **Time:** cooking time for soaked beans drops sharply within the first 3 hours
    of soaking and levels off after about 6.[^bassett]
- **Solute:** sugar draws water more slowly than salt, because sucrose stays a
    single molecule in solution while salt dissociates into two ions.[^macerate]

## Methods

- [Dry-brining](../methods/curing-and-brining.md#dry-brining) -- the juice salt
    draws out dissolves it and goes back in.
- [Wet-brining](../methods/curing-and-brining.md#wet-brining) -- salt already
    dissolved gets in faster.
- [Curing](../methods/curing-and-brining.md#curing) -- salt draws out the water
    bacteria need.
- [Marinating](../methods/pickling-and-fermenting.md#marinating) -- flavor
    reaches only the surface.
- [Pickling](../methods/pickling-and-fermenting.md#pickling) -- acid has to
    reach a safe level throughout.
- [Macerating](../methods/pickling-and-fermenting.md#macerating) -- sugar draws
    juice out of fruit.
- [Soaking grains](../methods/soaking.md#soaking-grains) and
    [soaking legumes](../methods/soaking.md#soaking-legumes) -- water moves in,
    and what it dissolves can be poured off.
- [Sweating](../methods/cooking-in-fat.md#sweating) -- salt draws water out of
    vegetables and keeps the pan wet.

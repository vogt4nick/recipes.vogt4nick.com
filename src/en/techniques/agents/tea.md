# Tea

Black and green tea are rich in tannins, polyphenols that bind the proteins in
saliva.

- **[Astringency](../perception/astringency.md):** tea's polyphenols bind
    saliva's proline-rich proteins and precipitate them, and the mouth loses its
    slick coating.[^wang] Milk's casein binds them first, which leaves less to
    dry the mouth.[^piyasena]
- **[Extraction](../mechanics/extraction.md):** brewing at 100ºC (212ºF) rather
    than 70ºC (158ºF) pulls more catechins from the leaf, and the tea turns more
    bitter and astringent.[^deng]

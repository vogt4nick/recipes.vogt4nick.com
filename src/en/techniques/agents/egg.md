# Egg

The white is mostly protein that sets and foams. The yolk carries an emulsifier.

- **[Aeration](../mechanics/aeration.md):** egg white proteins unfold and line
    up around each bubble, then bond to each other.[^soc] Copper binds to one of
    them, conalbumin, and makes overbeating less likely.[^mcgee] See
    [whipping egg whites](../methods/mixing-methods.md#whipping-egg-whites).
- **[Denaturation](../mechanics/denaturation.md):** the pH of egg white changes
    the temperature at which it coagulates.[^croguennec] See
    [acid in poaching liquid](../methods/moist-heat-cooking.md#acid-in-poaching-liquid).
- **[Emulsification](../mechanics/emulsification.md):** the yolk's low-density
    lipoproteins come apart at the surface of each oil droplet and coat
    it.[^mine] See
    [egg emulsification](../methods/stocks-and-sauces.md#egg-emulsification).
- **[Gluten development](../mechanics/gluten-development.md):** egg weakens a
    dough, so enriched doughs knead longer.[^hamel]

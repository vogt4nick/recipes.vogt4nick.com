# Acid

Vinegar, citrus juice, cream of tartar, and the acid that bacteria make in a
ferment all lower the pH of what they touch.

- **[Denaturation](../mechanics/denaturation.md):** the pH of egg white changes
    the temperature at which it coagulates.[^croguennec] See
    [acid in poaching liquid](../methods/moist-heat-cooking.md#acid-in-poaching-liquid).
- **[Aeration](../mechanics/aeration.md):** about 1/8 t cream of tartar or 1/2 t
    lemon juice per white steadies the foam.[^soc]
- **[Diffusion](../mechanics/diffusion.md):** an acidic marinade weakens
    collagen where it reaches, and left too long, it turns the surface
    mushy.[^atk-marinade] A pickle's acid inhibits microorganisms.[^uga] See
    [pickling and fermenting](../methods/pickling-and-fermenting.md).
- **[Extraction](../mechanics/extraction.md):** vinegar's low pH keeps
    anthocyanins bright[^onion] and turns garlic blue-green.[^greening] A lower
    pH raises the yield of gelatin from bone.[^gelatin] See
    [infusing vinegar](../methods/infusing.md#infusing-vinegar).
- **[Caramelization](../mechanics/caramelization.md):** lemon juice in the sugar
    keeps a caramel from crystallizing.[^lam]
- **[Fermentation](../mechanics/fermentation.md):** lactic acid breaks up the
    casein in cream and rejoins it into a network that thickens it.[^mccord] See
    [culturing](../methods/pickling-and-fermenting.md#culturing).

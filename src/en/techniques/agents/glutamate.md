# Glutamate

MSG, soy sauce, parmesan, tomato, kombu, and fish sauce are all sources of free
glutamate, the amino acid that tastes of umami.

- **[Umami synergy](../mechanics/umami-synergy.md):** glutamate on its own is
    mild, and paired with inosinate or guanylate its umami grows about
    eightfold.[^kurihara] A long-aged cheese has more of it, because enzymes
    keep breaking its milk protein down into free amino acids.[^uic-cheese]

[Ramen](../../recipes/soups/ramen.md) adds MSG to chicken stock, whose chicken
brings the inosinate.

[^kurihara]: Kurihara, Kenzo.
    ["Umami the Fifth Basic Taste: History of Studies on Receptor Mechanisms and Role as a Food Flavor."](https://doi.org/10.1155/2015/189402)

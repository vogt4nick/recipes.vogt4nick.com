# Sugar

Heated alone, sugar becomes caramel. In everything else it feeds yeast or pulls
on water.

- **[Caramelization](../mechanics/caramelization.md):** sugar heated on its own
    breaks apart into new compounds that bring color, flavor, and
    aroma.[^atk-caramel] See [cooking sugar](../methods/cooking-sugar.md).
- **[Fermentation](../mechanics/fermentation.md):** yeast feeds on sugar and
    gives off carbon dioxide and alcohol.[^redstar]
- **[Diffusion](../mechanics/diffusion.md):** sugar draws water out of fruit
    more slowly than salt, because sucrose stays a single molecule in
    solution.[^macerate] See
    [macerating](../methods/pickling-and-fermenting.md#macerating).
- **[Aeration](../mechanics/aeration.md):** the jagged edges of sugar crystals
    drag air into butter as the two are beaten together.[^exploratorium] See
    [creaming](../methods/mixing-methods.md#creaming).
- **[Gluten development](../mechanics/gluten-development.md):** sugar weakens a
    dough, so enriched doughs knead longer.[^hamel]

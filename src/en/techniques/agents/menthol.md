# Menthol

Peppermint, spearmint, and their oils cool the mouth with menthol.

- **[Chemesthesis](../mechanics/chemesthesis.md):** menthol opens TRPM8, the
    receptor that senses cold, so mint feels cool at room temperature.[^mckemy]

[^mckemy]: McKemy, D. D., W. M. Neuhausser, and D. Julius.
    ["Identification of a Cold Receptor Reveals a General Role for TRP Channels in Thermosensation."](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature719)
    _Nature_ 416: 52-58. 2002.

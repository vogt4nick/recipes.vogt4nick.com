# Tannins

Black tea, red wine, walnuts, and pomegranate are rich in tannins, polyphenols
that bind the proteins in saliva.

- **[Astringency](../mechanics/astringency.md):** tannins bind saliva's
    proline-rich proteins and precipitate them, and the mouth loses its slick
    coating.[^wang] Milk's casein binds the polyphenols in tea first, which
    leaves less to dry the mouth.[^piyasena]
- **[Extraction](../mechanics/extraction.md):** a high-proof spirit pulls bitter
    and astringent compounds that a weaker one leaves behind.[^ethanol] See
    [steeping in alcohol](../methods/infusing.md#steeping-in-alcohol).

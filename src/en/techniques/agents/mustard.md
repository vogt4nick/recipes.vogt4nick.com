# Mustard

Brown and black mustard seed, along with horseradish and wasabi, get their
sinus-clearing sting from allyl isothiocyanate, which forms only once their
cells are crushed.

- **[Spiciness](../perception/spiciness.md):** allyl isothiocyanate opens TRPA1,
    a different channel from the one chili opens.[^jordt]
- **[Extraction](../mechanics/extraction.md):** the seed keeps its
    glucosinolates apart from the enzyme myrosinase until its cells are damaged,
    and heat denatures the enzyme.[^lietzow] Cold water makes the hottest
    mustard from powder.[^carpenter] See
    [blooming spices](../methods/infusing.md#blooming-spices).
- **[Emulsification](../mechanics/emulsification.md):** the mucilage around the
    seed hulls holds a vinaigrette's oil and vinegar together.[^atk-mustard] See
    [mustard emulsification](../methods/stocks-and-sauces.md#mustard-emulsification).

[Masoor dal tadka](../../recipes/legumes/lentils/masoor-dal-tadka.md) fries
whole black mustard seed in hot oil, which denatures the enzyme before the seed
is ever crushed in water.

    ["Mustard Oils and Cannabinoids Excite Sensory Nerve Fibres Through the TRP Channel ANKTM1."](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02282)
    _Nature_ 427 (6971): 260-265. 2004.

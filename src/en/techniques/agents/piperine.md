# Piperine

Black, white, and long pepper get their bite from piperine.

- **[Chemesthesis](../mechanics/chemesthesis.md):** piperine opens TRPV1, the
    heat receptor that capsaicin opens, and TRPA1, the one behind mustard's
    sting.[^okumura]

[^okumura]: Okumura, Y., M. Narukawa, Y. Iwasaki, A. Ishikawa, H. Matsuda, M.
    Yoshikawa, and T. Watanabe.
    ["Activation of TRPV1 and TRPA1 by Black Pepper Components."](https://doi.org/10.1271/bbb.90964)
    _Bioscience, Biotechnology, and Biochemistry_ 74 (5): 1068-1072.
    2010\.

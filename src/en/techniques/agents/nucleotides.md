# Nucleotides

Meat and dried fish supply inosinate, dried shiitake supply guanylate, and the
I+G powder sold alongside MSG is a blend of the two.

- **[Umami synergy](../mechanics/umami-synergy.md):** a nucleotide binds T1R1
    near the opening of glutamate's pocket and holds the receptor in its closed,
    active shape.[^zhang] Glutamate's umami grows about eightfold.[^kurihara]
    Inosinate forms as ATP breaks down in meat and fish after slaughter.
    Guanylate is scarce in raw mushrooms and plentiful in dried ones.[^kurihara]
    See [stock](../methods/stocks-and-sauces.md#stock).

[^zhang]: Zhang, F., B. Klebansky, R. M. Fine, H. Xu, A. Pronin, H. Liu, C.
    Tachdjian, and X. Li.

    _Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences_ 105 (52):
    20930-20934. 2008.

[^kurihara]: Kurihara, Kenzo.

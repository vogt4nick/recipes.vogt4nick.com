# Allyl isothiocyanate

Wasabi, horseradish, and brown and black mustard seed get their sinus-clearing
sting from allyl isothiocyanate, which forms only once their cells are crushed.

- **[Chemesthesis](../mechanics/chemesthesis.md):** allyl isothiocyanate opens
    TRPA1, a different channel from the one chili opens.[^jordt]
- **[Extraction](../mechanics/extraction.md):** the seed keeps its
    glucosinolates apart from the enzyme myrosinase until its cells are damaged,
    and heat denatures the enzyme.[^lietzow] Cold water makes the hottest
    mustard from powder.[^carpenter] See
    [blooming spices](../methods/infusing.md#blooming-spices).

[Masoor dal tadka](../../recipes/legumes/lentils/masoor-dal-tadka.md) fries
whole black mustard seed in hot oil, which denatures the enzyme before the seed
is ever crushed in water.

[^jordt]: Jordt, S.-E., D. M. Bautista, H.-H. Chuang, D. D. McKemy, P. M.
    Zygmunt, E. D. Högestätt, I. D. Meng, and D. Julius.
    ["Mustard Oils and Cannabinoids Excite Sensory Nerve Fibres Through the TRP Channel ANKTM1."](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02282)
    _Nature_ 427 (6971): 260-265. 2004.

    ANKTM1 is the channel now called TRPA1.

[^lietzow]: Lietzow, J.
    ["Biologically Active Compounds in Mustard Seeds: A Toxicological Perspective."](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods10092089)
    _Foods_ 10 (9): 2089. 2021.

[^carpenter]: Carpenter, Dave.

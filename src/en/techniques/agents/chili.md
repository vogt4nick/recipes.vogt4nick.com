# Chili

Fresh and dried chili peppers, chili flakes, cayenne, and gochugaru all owe
their heat to capsaicin.

- **[Spiciness](../perception/spiciness.md):** capsaicin opens TRPV1, the
    receptor that fires at painful heat.[^caterina] Skim milk puts out the burn
    as well as whole milk does, so the relief doesn't come from the
    fat.[^nolden]
- **[Extraction](../mechanics/extraction.md):** capsaicin dissolves readily in
    fat and barely at all in water.[^oil] See
    [infusing oils](../methods/infusing.md#infusing-oils).

[^caterina]: Caterina, M. J., M. A. Schumacher, M. Tominaga, T. A. Rosen, J. D.
    Levine, and D. Julius.
    ["The Capsaicin Receptor: A Heat-Activated Ion Channel in the Pain Pathway."](https://doi.org/10.1038/39807)
    _Nature_ 389 (6653): 816-824. 1997.

[^nolden]: Nolden, A. A., G. Lenart, and J. E. Hayes.
    ["Putting Out the Fire: Efficacy of Common Beverages in Reducing Oral Burn from Capsaicin."](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physbeh.2019.05.018)
    _Physiology & Behavior_ 208: 112557. 2019.

    The authors leave the mechanism open among fat, protein, and sugar.

[^oil]: Lersch, Martin.
    ["Wonders of Extraction: Oil."](https://khymos.org/?p=436) _Khymos._ 24
    October 2008.

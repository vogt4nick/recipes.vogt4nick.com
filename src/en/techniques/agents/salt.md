# Salt

Salt moves water, and that one property reaches into five mechanics.

- **[Diffusion](../mechanics/diffusion.md):** salt on food draws water out by
    osmosis,[^rothman] and given time, the salty juice is drawn back in.[^earl]
    See [curing and brining](../methods/curing-and-brining.md).
- **[Denaturation](../mechanics/denaturation.md):** salt changes meat's muscle
    proteins so they hold on to more water through cooking.[^bomze]
- **[Pectin breakdown](../mechanics/pectin-breakdown.md):** sodium takes the
    place of some of the calcium and magnesium holding a bean skin's pectin
    together.[^atk-beans] See
    [brining beans](../methods/curing-and-brining.md#brining-beans).
- **[Fermentation](../mechanics/fermentation.md):** about 2% of the flour's
    weight slows yeast without killing it.[^battilana]
- **[Gluten development](../mechanics/gluten-development.md):** salt tightens
    the gluten so the dough holds carbon dioxide better.[^kasalt]

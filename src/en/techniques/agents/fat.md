# Fat

Fat carries heat past the boiling point of water and dissolves what water can't.

- **[Maillard reaction](../mechanics/maillard-reaction.md):** oil shimmers at
    about 135ºC (275ºF) and starts to smoke at about 204ºC (400ºF).[^atk] See
    [cooking in fat](../methods/cooking-in-fat.md).
- **[Pectin breakdown](../mechanics/pectin-breakdown.md):** in fat at 82-107ºC
    (180-225ºF), vegetables soften without frying.[^allclad] See
    [confit](../methods/cooking-in-fat.md#confit).
- **[Extraction](../mechanics/extraction.md):** oil is the solvent for most
    aroma compounds.[^ethanol] See
    [infusing oils](../methods/infusing.md#infusing-oils).
- **[Gluten development](../mechanics/gluten-development.md):** fat coats the
    gluten-forming proteins, so little gluten forms once the liquid goes
    in.[^exploratorium-gluten] See
    [limiting gluten](../methods/mixing-methods.md#limiting-gluten).
- **[Aeration](../mechanics/aeration.md):** a trace of fat keeps egg whites from
    foaming,[^soc] while partly crystallized fat is what holds whipped cream
    up.[^fredrick]
- **[Emulsification](../mechanics/emulsification.md):** butter whisked into a
    little warm liquid emulsifies, and splits if the sauce simmers.[^fdl] See
    [mounting with butter](../methods/stocks-and-sauces.md#mounting-with-butter).

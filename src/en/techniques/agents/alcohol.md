# Alcohol

Ethanol is polar at one end and oily at the other, so it dissolves what water
and fat each leave behind.[^ethanol]

- **[Extraction](../mechanics/extraction.md):** ethanol dissolves both taste and
    aroma compounds, and a high-proof spirit pulls bitter and astringent
    compounds that a weaker one leaves behind.[^ethanol] Eggnog inoculated with
    salmonella tested sterile after three weeks under refrigeration.[^nog] See
    [steeping in alcohol](../methods/infusing.md#steeping-in-alcohol).
- **[Gluten development](../mechanics/gluten-development.md):** gluten doesn't
    form in alcohol.[^lopezalt]
- **[Fermentation](../mechanics/fermentation.md):** yeast makes it, along with
    carbon dioxide.[^redstar] See
    [brewing](../methods/pickling-and-fermenting.md#brewing).

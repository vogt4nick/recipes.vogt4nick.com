# Velveting

Meat for a stir-fry is marinated with water, seasoning, cornstarch, and oil,
then par-cooked in oil or water before it meets the wok. That gives it a silky
texture, keeps the marinade's moisture and flavor in, and protects it from the
hot wok.[^leung]

# Slurry

Cornstarch stirred into an equal amount of water and drizzled into a sauce
thickens it without powdery lumps. Heat does the thickening, so let the sauce
come back to a simmer before judging it; cooked too long, the starch breaks down
and the sauce thins again. Starch settles out of the water quickly, so re-stir
the slurry right before it goes in.[^leung]

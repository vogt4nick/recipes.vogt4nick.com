# Pasta water emulsification

Pasta cooking water carries starch, and starch keeps cheese from clumping. In a
sauce of grated cheese and water, starch concentration is the key factor in
stability; with starch below about 1% of the cheese's weight, the sauce seizes
into clumps, the "Mozzarella phase."[^bartolucci]

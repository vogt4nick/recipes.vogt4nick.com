# Roux

A roux's color trades thickening for flavor. Against a white roux cooked 1
minute, a blond roux cooked 3 minutes has 14% less thickening power, and a
peanut butter-colored roux cooked 5 minutes has 26% less. The heat of frying
flour in fat breaks down its starch chains, while the darker the roux, the more
pronounced its flavor.[^atk]

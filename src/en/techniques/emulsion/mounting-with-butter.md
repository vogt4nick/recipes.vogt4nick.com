# Mounting with butter

Butter whisked into a little warm liquid emulsifies. Keep the sauce below a
simmer; if bubbles form, pull it off the heat before the emulsion splits.[^fdl]

## Where it's used

- [Brown duxelles](../../recipes/sauces/espag%C2%ADnol/brown-duxelles.md) --
    finishes by stirring in butter.
- [Hollandaise](../../recipes/sauces/hollandaise/index.md) -- whisks butter into
    warm yolks.

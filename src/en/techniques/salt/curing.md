# Curing

Curing preserves meat, fish, or olives with salt, sometimes mixed with sugar and
spices. The salt draws moisture out, leaving bacteria less to grow on, and works
into the food, where it makes conditions hostile to them.[^cooksinfo]

[Cured egg yolks](../../recipes/seasonings/cured-egg-yolk.md) sit 5-7 days in
salt and sugar, then dry in a low oven.

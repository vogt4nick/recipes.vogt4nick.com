# Sweating

Salt added to vegetables as they start cooking draws their moisture out by
osmosis,[^rothman] so they soften. Once that moisture has cooked off in the pan,
they can brown.

## Where it's used

- [Caramelized onions](../../recipes/vegetables/caramelized-onions.md)
- [Loaded french onion soup](../../recipes/soups/loaded-french-onion-soup.md) --
    built on caramelized onions.
- [Mushroom gravy](../../recipes/sauces/espag%C2%ADnol/mushroom-gravy.md) --
    salts mushrooms and cooks them until their liquid is released.
- [Hot bacon vinaigrette](../../recipes/sauces/vinaigrette/mustard-based/hot-bacon-vinaigrette.md)
    -- sautés onion with salt.

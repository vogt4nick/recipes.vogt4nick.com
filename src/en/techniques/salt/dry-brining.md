# Dry-brining

Salt applied to raw meat well ahead of cooking draws the juices inside to the
surface. The salt dissolves in them, and that brine is eventually reabsorbed.
The salt also changes the muscle proteins so they hold on to more of their own
juices. Left uncovered overnight, the skin dries out too, so it crisps.[^earl]

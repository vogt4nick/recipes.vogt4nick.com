# Culturing

A bacterial culture added to cream turns its lactose into lactic acid, which
sours and thickens it. Crème fraîche has more fat and less protein than sour
cream, so it can be cooked without curdling.[^mccord]

[Crème fraîche](../../recipes/sauces/creme-fraiche.md) rests 24 hours in a warm
spot, about 22ºC (75ºF), then chills 24 hours.

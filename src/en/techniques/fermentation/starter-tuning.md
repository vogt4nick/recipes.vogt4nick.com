# Starter tuning

A sourdough starter is two populations with different needs. The yeast _Candida
milleri_ grows best at 27ºC (81ºF), cooler than the lactobacilli it lives with.
_Lactobacillus sanfranciscensis_ grows only between pH 3.9 and 6.7 and stops at
4% salt, while the yeast tolerates pH 3.5-7 and up to 8% salt.[^ganzle]
Temperature, acidity, and salt shift the balance between them.

The [starter](../../recipes/breads/leaven-breads/starter.md) is fed with water
at 29-32ºC (85-90ºF).

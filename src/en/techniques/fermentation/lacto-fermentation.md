# Lacto-fermentation

Lactic acid bacteria sour and preserve vegetables. In commercial sauerkraut the
main species are _Leuconostoc mesenteroides_ and _Lactobacillus plantarum_,
alongside minor species, and lowering the salt may change which ones
grow.[^plengvidhya]

## Where it's used

No recipe is lacto-fermented yet. [Kvass](../../recipes/beverages/kvass.md) and
[fermented lemonade](../../recipes/beverages/fermented-lemonade.md) ferment with
yeast; the lactobacilli in the
[starter](../../recipes/breads/leaven-breads/starter.md) are the nearest.

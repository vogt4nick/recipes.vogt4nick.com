# Fermentation

Temperature, salt, and acidity decide which microbes dominate a ferment. In
sourdough, the yeast _Candida milleri_ grows best at 27ºC (81ºF), cooler than
its partner _Lactobacillus sanfranciscensis_. The bacterium stops growing at 4%
salt; the yeast tolerates 8%.[^ganzle] In sauerkraut, the work falls to lactic
acid bacteria such as _Leuconostoc mesenteroides_ and _Lactobacillus
plantarum_.[^plengvidhya]

    _Applied and Environmental Microbiology_ 64 (7): 2616-2623. 1998.

# Moist-heat cooking

Moist-heat methods carry heat to the food through water or a water-based
liquid.[^gisslen] Water holds a surface near 100ºC (212ºF), too cool for the
[Maillard reaction](../mechanics/maillard-reaction.md), so nothing cooked in it
browns.[^aoki]

## Poaching

Water held below a boil caps the temperature of whatever cooks in it. Poaching
water sits at 71-82ºC (160-180ºF),[^wca] where proteins set by
[denaturation](../mechanics/denaturation.md).

[Poached chicken breast](../../recipes/meats/poultry/poached-chicken-breast.md)
holds the water at 80ºC (175ºF) and stops when the breast reads 68ºC (155ºF);
[poached shrimp](../../recipes/meats/seafood/poached-shrimp.md) come out at 60ºC
(140ºF).

## Acid in poaching liquid

Vinegar in the poaching water lowers the pH the white meets as it sets around
the yolk, which changes the temperature at which it
[denatures](../mechanics/denaturation.md).[^croguennec]

[Poached eggs](../../recipes/eggs/poached-eggs.md) rinse in cold water afterward
to wash off the lingering vinegar.

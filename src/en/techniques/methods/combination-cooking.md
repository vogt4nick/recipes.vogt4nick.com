# Combination cooking

Combination methods brown food in dry heat, then finish it in liquid. The liquid
never gets hot enough to brown anything, so the color has to come first.

## Braising

A braise cooks a tough cut in liquid just below a boil until its collagen
dissolves into gelatin, a slow kind of
[denaturation](../mechanics/denaturation.md). Hotter liquid squeezes more juice
out of the meat and dries it.[^blais] The sear before it is the only chance at
the [Maillard reaction](../mechanics/maillard-reaction.md), and
[deglazing](stocks-and-sauces.md#deglazing) carries the browned bits into the
braise.

[Braised beef](../../recipes/meats/beef/braised-beef.md),
[osso buco](../../recipes/meats/pork/osso-buco.md), and
[sauerbraten](../../recipes/meats/beef/sauerbraten.md) sear the meat before it
goes into liquid that will never get hot enough to brown it.

# Mixing methods

How a batter or pastry is mixed decides how much air it holds by
[aeration](../mechanics/aeration.md) and how much
[gluten](../mechanics/gluten-development.md) it builds. Most of the work after
the beating goes into keeping the air and keeping the gluten down.

## Creaming

Beaten with sugar, butter turns pale and light as the jagged edges of the sugar
crystals drag air into the fat. Creaming takes 2 to 4 minutes, depending on the
temperature of the ingredients and the mixer.[^exploratorium] Baking powder and
soda don't make bubbles of their own -- the carbon dioxide they give off
collects in the air cells that creaming made.[^exploratorium] Skimp on creaming
and the leavener has less to fill.

[Yellow cake](../../recipes/desserts/cakes/yellow-cake.md),
[Creamed chocolate cake](../../recipes/desserts/cakes/chocolate-cake/creamed-chocolate-cake.md),
and six cookie recipes cream butter and sugar until fluffy.
[Russian buttercream](../../recipes/desserts/toppings/russian-buttercream.md)
whips butter alone until aerated before the condensed milk goes in.

## Whipping egg whites

Beating unfolds egg white proteins, which line up around each bubble with their
water-repelling side in the air and their water-loving side in the liquid, then
bond to each other.[^soc] That's [denaturation](../mechanics/denaturation.md) at
the surface of a bubble. The copper in a copper bowl binds to conalbumin, an egg
white protein, and makes overbeating less likely.[^mcgee]

[Kaiserschmarrn](../../recipes/breads/pancakes/kaiserschmarrn.md) beats whites
with lemon juice to soft peaks.
[Yellow cake](../../recipes/desserts/cakes/yellow-cake.md) whips them with cream
of tartar to stiff peaks.
[Toasted marshmallow butterscotch pie](../../recipes/desserts/pies/toasted-marshmallow-butterscotch-pie.md)
whips a cream of tartar meringue until voluminous and shiny.

## Ribbon stage

Yolks and sugar beaten together thicken, turn pale, and fall from the whisk in
ribbons that rest on the surface before sinking. Whisking over a water bath gets
there sooner; sabayon is yolks and sugar whisked to ribbon over gentle
heat.[^cooksinfo]

[Chocolate mousse](../../recipes/desserts/chocolate-mousse.md) whisks its yolks
over heat until thick, foamy, and 63 to 65ºC.
[Citrus curd tart](../../recipes/desserts/tarts/citrus-curd-tart.md) foams whole
eggs with sugar before the juice goes in, and
[Tortilla Española](../../recipes/eggs/tortilla-espanola.md) beats its eggs
until frothy, twice.

## Whipping cream

Whipping knocks partly crystallized fat globules together. The crystals keep
them from merging into one drop, so they stick into a network around the
bubbles. This is partial coalescence, and it's what gives whipped toppings their
structure.[^fredrick]

[Chocolate mousse](../../recipes/desserts/chocolate-mousse.md) whips cream to
medium stiff peaks and warns against going further.
[Cheesecake](../../recipes/desserts/cheesecake/index.md) whips cold cream 4-5
minutes to stiff peaks.

## Folding

Air that took minutes to beat in is gone in a few strokes of a whisk. To combine
a foam with a heavier base, fold with a spatula: cut down through the middle,
sweep along the bottom, and lift, turning the bowl as you go. Stir about a third
of the foam into the base first to lighten it, then fold in the rest.

[Chocolate mousse](../../recipes/desserts/chocolate-mousse.md) folds a third of
the chocolate into the cream first, then the rest, "keeping as much air in the
mixture as possible." [Cheesecake](../../recipes/desserts/cheesecake/index.md)
folds slowly to preserve the air in its whipped cream.

## Limiting foam

Sometimes foam is the problem.
[Aged egg nog](../../recipes/beverages/aged-egg-nog.md) keeps the stick blender
submerged so it doesn't whip air into the yolks.

## Limiting gluten

Gluten makes pie crust tough and chewy.[^anastopoulo] Cutting fat into flour
coats some of the flour, which can't absorb water, and only the uncoated flour
forms gluten.[^lopezalt]

[Pie crust](../../recipes/breads/pastry-doughs/pie-crust.md) cuts its butter in
before any water goes in, and stops working the dough as soon as it holds
together.

## Resting dough

A rest relaxes the gluten, so the dough stretches instead of springing
back.[^exploratorium-gluten]

[Strudel dough](../../recipes/breads/pastry-doughs/strudel-dough.md) is kneaded
for strength, then rests before it's stretched thin enough to read a newspaper
through.

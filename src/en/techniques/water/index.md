# Water

Water dissolves the compounds that make food taste salty, sweet, sour, and
bitter; most aromas dissolve in oil instead. Shake oil into a clear meat stock
and let it settle, and the water tastes salty and faintly meaty while the oil
carries the meaty aroma.[^lersch] Water also boils at about 100ºC (212ºF), which
caps the temperature of anything wet. Browning needs about 150ºC (300ºF), so a
wet surface won't brown until its water is gone.[^aoki]

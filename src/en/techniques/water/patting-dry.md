# Patting dry

A wet surface won't get hot enough to brown until all its water has evaporated,
so wet meat steams and turns gray first.[^aoki] Blotting it with paper towels
removes that water before the heat has to boil it off.

## Where it's used

- [BBRRR chicken breast](../../recipes/meats/poultry/oven-roasted-chicken/bbrrr-chicken-breast.md)
    -- after brining.
- [Oven roasted duck](../../recipes/meats/poultry/oven-roasted-duck/index.md) --
    after rinsing.
- [Sauerbraten](../../recipes/meats/beef/sauerbraten.md) -- after three days in

- [Hash browns](../../recipes/vegetables/potatoes/hash-browns.md) -- squeezes
    the shredded potato dry.

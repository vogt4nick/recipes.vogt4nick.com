# Fat

Many of the compounds that give spices their flavor, such as terpenes, phenols,
and essential oils, dissolve in fat, not water. Hot fat extracts them and
carries them through a dish.[^sarasin] How much of an aroma reaches you depends
both on the fat and on how fat-soluble the aroma compound is.[^relkin]

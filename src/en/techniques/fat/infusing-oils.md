# Infusing oils

Oil warmed with aromatics dissolves their fat-soluble flavor
compounds,[^sarasin] which stay behind after the solids are strained out.

[Garlic](../../recipes/seasonings/infused-oils/garlic-infused-olive-oil.md),
[lemon](../../recipes/seasonings/infused-oils/lemon-infused-olive-oil.md), and
[pizza crust](../../recipes/seasonings/infused-oils/pizza-crust-oil.md) oils
warm their aromatics 5 minutes over medium-low heat, then steep 2 hours off the
heat before straining.

!!! warning "Refrigerate garlic oil."

    Low-acid garlic-in-oil can grow _Clostridium botulinum_ and its toxin unless it
    is kept constantly refrigerated, which is why the FDA requires acidifying agents
    or microbial inhibitors in commercial versions.[^morse]

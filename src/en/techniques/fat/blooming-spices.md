# Blooming spices

Fat acts as a solvent for spices' fat-soluble compounds, so frying spices
briefly in hot fat, a _tadka_, pulls their flavor into the fat, which then
carries it through the dish. Spices bloom when they hit fat at about 180-200ºC
(356-392ºF).[^sarasin]

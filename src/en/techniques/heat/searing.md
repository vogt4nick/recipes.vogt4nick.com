# Searing

A seared crust is the Maillard reaction, which happens around 150ºC (300ºF). A
wet surface is held near 100ºC (212ºF) while its water boils off, so it steams
and turns gray before it can brown.[^aoki] The pan goes to 204-232ºC (400-450ºF)
before the meat goes in.[^earl]

A sauce browns the same way. Soy sauce poured down the hot side of a wok browns
as its water boils off against the metal; poured into the food, it sits among
wet ingredients near 100ºC (212ºF), and the stir-fry loses its _wok hei_.[^lieu]

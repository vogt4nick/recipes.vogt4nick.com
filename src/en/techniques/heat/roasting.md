# Roasting

Roasting depends on dry heat reaching every piece so its surface can dry out and
brown. Food releases moisture as it cooks, and pieces packed together trap it
and steam.[^mcneill]

Most oven roasted vegetables, like
[brussels sprouts](../../recipes/vegetables/oven-roasted-vegetables/oven-roasted-brussels-sprouts.md),
go in at 232ºC (450ºF).

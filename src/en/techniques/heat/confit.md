# Confit

Confit cooks food submerged in fat, low and slow, usually 82-107ºC (180-225ºF),
until tender. Vegetables soften without losing their structure. Fat that gets
too hot fries the food.[^allclad]

## Where it's used

- [Confit cabbage](../../recipes/vegetables/confit-cabbage.md) -- bastes in
    butter in a 120ºC (250ºF) oven.

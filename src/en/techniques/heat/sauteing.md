# Sautéing

Sautéing cooks food quickly in a small amount of fat over high heat, tossing or
flipping it in the pan as it cooks.[^gisslen] Oil shimmers at about 135ºC
(275ºF), hot enough for vegetables, and starts to smoke at about 204ºC (400ºF),
the cue for meat.[^atk]

## Where it's used

- [Garlic shrimp](../../recipes/meats/seafood/garlic-shrimp.md)
- [Cashew chicken](../../recipes/meats/poultry/cashew-chicken.md)
- [Quesadillas](../../recipes/sandwiches/quesadillas.md) -- sautés the peppers
    over high heat.
- [Beef bowl](../../recipes/meats/beef/beef-bowl.md) -- sautés the vegetables.

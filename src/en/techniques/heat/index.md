# Heat

Meat's final temperature strongly affects its tenderness and how much weight it
loses. Beef heated slowly barely changes in tenderness for 4 hours, then softens
sharply as it warms from 50ºC (122ºF) to 60ºC (140ºF), while its weight loss
climbs steadily.[^laakkonen] Veal softens as cooking temperature rises from 50ºC
(122ºF) to 60ºC (140ºF), driven by changes in connective tissue.[^bouton] High
heat dries the surface and browns it.

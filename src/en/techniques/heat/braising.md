# Braising

A braise cooks a tough cut in liquid just below a boil, 85-90ºC (185-195ºF),
until its collagen dissolves into gelatin. At that temperature the collagen
needs 2-3 hours, depending on the size of the cut. Hotter liquid squeezes more
juice out of the meat and dries it.[^blais]

# Sweating

Sweating cooks vegetables slowly in a little fat over low heat until they soften
and turn translucent, without browning.[^nyorani] Salt draws their water out by
osmosis,[^rothman] and a lid keeps that water in the pan. While the pan stays
wet, the vegetables can't get much hotter than 100ºC (212ºF), well short of what
browning needs. Color in the pan means the heat is too high.

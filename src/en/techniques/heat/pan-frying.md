# Pan-frying

Pan-frying cooks food in just enough oil to cover the bottom of the
pan.[^remley] The food stays in place rather than being tossed, usually for
longer and at lower heat than a sauté.[^gisslen] The oil runs about 170ºC
(340ºF) for meat and 190ºC (375ºF) for vegetables.[^remley]

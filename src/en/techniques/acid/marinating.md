# Marinating

An acidic marinade weakens collagen, but only at the surface. Red wine
penetrates less than 1 mm into beef short ribs in 18 hours, and acid left too
long turns the outermost layer mushy, not tender.[^atk] Because a marinade works
on the surface, it matters most on small or thin pieces.

## Where it's used

- [Sauerbraten](../../recipes/meats/beef/sauerbraten.md) -- vinegar, 3 days.
- [Chicken schwarma](../../recipes/meats/poultry/chicken-schwarma.md) -- lemon
    juice and red wine vinegar.
- [Chicken satay](../../recipes/meats/poultry/chicken-satay.md) -- lime juice or
    rice vinegar.

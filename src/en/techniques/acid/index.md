# Acid

Acid changes how proteins behave. The pH of egg white changes the temperature at
which it coagulates.[^croguennec] Acid also weakens collagen in meat, but only
where it touches: red wine penetrates less than 1 mm into beef in 18 hours, and
acid left too long turns the outer layer mushy, not tender.[^atk] In a pickle,
acidity governs safety as well as taste and texture.[^uga]

# Deglazing

The browned bits stuck to a pan after searing, the fond, are concentrated
flavor, and hot liquid dissolves them. Water, stock, wine, and vinegar all
work.[^falkowitz] Wine or vinegar also adds acid to the sauce.

## Where it's used

- [Caramelized onions](../../recipes/vegetables/caramelized-onions.md) -- wine
    or water.
- [Beef bowl](../../recipes/meats/beef/beef-bowl.md) -- Shaoxing wine or vinegar
    in the sauce.
- [Osso buco](../../recipes/meats/pork/osso-buco.md) -- white wine.
- [Baked beans](../../recipes/legumes/beans/baked-beans.md) -- beer.
- [Braised beef](../../recipes/meats/beef/braised-beef.md) -- stock.
- [Pan sauce](../../recipes/sauces/espag%C2%ADnol/pan-sauce.md) -- stock.
- [Orange marmalade pan sauce](../../recipes/meats/poultry/oven-roasted-duck/orange-marmalde-pan-sauce.md)
    -- water and duck juices.
- [Cashew coconut curry](../../recipes/curries/cashew-coconut-curry.md) --
    coconut milk.

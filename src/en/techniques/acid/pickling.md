# Pickling

A pickle's acidity governs its safety as well as its taste and texture, which is
why tested recipes call for vinegar of 5% acidity in the stated amount. The acid
inhibits microorganisms and denatures the enzymes that cause spoilage.[^uga]

[Pickled ginger](../../recipes/seasonings/pickles/pickled-ginger.md) and
[pickled beetroot](../../recipes/seasonings/pickles/pickled-beetroot.md) are
refrigerator pickles and keep up to two weeks chilled.

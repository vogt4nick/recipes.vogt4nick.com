# Sensory-specific satiety

A food grows less pleasant as it's eaten, while foods not yet eaten keep their
appeal. The drop is specific to the food that was eaten.[^rolls]

## Levers

- **Variety:** a second food unlike the first is still pleasant after the first
    has stopped being so.[^rolls]
- **Astringents:** tea, wine, and pickles eaten with fatty food make it seem
    less fatty.[^peyrot]

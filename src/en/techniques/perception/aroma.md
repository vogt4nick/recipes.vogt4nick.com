# Aroma

Smell reaches the nose two ways: sniffed from outside, and drawn up from the
mouth while chewing. The second is so often mistaken for taste that smell is the
only major sense regularly confused with another, and many people dislike the
smell of a strong cheese yet like its taste.[^rozin]

## Levers

- **Solvent:** most aroma compounds dissolve in fat rather than water, so oil
    carries aroma where water carries taste.[^ethanol]
- **Fat:** how much of an aroma escapes a food depends on the kind of fat and on
    how fat-soluble the aroma compound is.[^relkin]

## Methods

- [Infusing oils](../methods/infusing.md#infusing-oils) -- oil takes up the
    aroma and keeps it once the solids are strained out.
- [Blooming spices](../methods/infusing.md#blooming-spices) -- hot fat pulls
    aroma out of spices fast.

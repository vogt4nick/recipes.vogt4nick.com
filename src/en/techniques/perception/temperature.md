# Temperature

Sweetness grows stronger as food warms. TRPM5, a channel in the taste cells that
sense sweet, opens more readily between 15 and 35ºC (59 and 95ºF), and the nerve
response to sugar rises across that range. Some people taste something when only
the tongue is heated or cooled, with nothing on it.[^talavera]

## Levers

- **Serving temperature:** a cold dessert or drink tastes less sweet than the
    same thing warm.[^talavera]
- **Cold rinse:** a rinse at 5ºC (41ºF) eases a capsaicin burn better than one
    at 20ºC (68ºF).[^nasrawi]
- **Mint:** menthol opens TRPM8, the receptor for cold, so mint feels cool at
    room temperature.[^mckemy]

=== "Ingredients"

    - 2 Pears, peeled, cored, chopped
    - 60 g Soy Sauce
    - 15 g Dark Soy Sauce
    - 45 g Gochujang or Ssamjang or BBQ Sauce
    - 30 g Ginger Root
    - 15 g Garlic
    - 15 g Toasted Sesame Oil
    - 10 g Rice Vinegar
    - 1 t Black Peppercorn, ground

=== "Directions"

    Blend ingredients until smooth.

!!! info "Use on steak or ground beef."

!!! tip "Add sauce only after browning, or the sugar scorches before the meat crusts."

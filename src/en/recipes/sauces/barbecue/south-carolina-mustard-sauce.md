# South Carolina mustard sauce

=== "Ingredients"

    - 1 t Neutral Oil
    - 3/4 t Onion Powder
    - 1/8 t Garlic Powder
    - 115 g Dijon Mustard
    - 75 g Brown Sugar, packed
    - 80 g Distilled White Vinegar
    - 55 g Water
    - 1/2 T Hot Sauce
    - 1/4 t Salt

    !!! info "Yields about 250 g."

=== "Directions"

    1. **Hydrate powders.** Stir onion powder and garlic powder into enough water to
        make a paste. Soak 1-2 minutes.
    2. **Bloom.** Warm oil in a small nonreactive saucepan over low heat. Add paste.
        Stir 20-30 seconds until fragrant and not browned.
    3. **Combine.** Add vinegar immediately. Whisk in mustard, sugar, remaining
        water, hot sauce, salt, and pepper until smooth.
    4. **Simmer** uncovered over medium-low heat 5-8 minutes.
    5. **Check volume.** Pour into a heatproof measuring jug. Target 250 ml. Return
        to pan and simmer 1-2 minutes more if over. Stir in water if under.
    6. **Adjust.** Taste. Add salt or hot sauce.
    7. **Cool and bottle.** Cool to room temperature. Sieve into bottle with a
        funnel.

!!! tip "Thin with vinegar, 1 t at a time, if too thick to squeeze."

# Eastern North Carolina vinegar sauce

=== "Ingredients"

    - 240 g
        [Black Peppercorn-Infused Apple Cider Vinegar](../../seasonings/infused-vinegar.md)
    - 16 g Chili Powder
    - 4 g Kosher Salt
    - 4 g Granulated Sugar
    - Hot Sauce

    !!! info "Yields approx 250 g"

=== "Directions"

    1. **Combine** all ingredients in a sealable container.
    2. **Shake** until the salt and sugar dissolve.
    3. **Rest** at least 30 minutes before serving.

!!! tip "For best flavor, make it a few hours ahead."

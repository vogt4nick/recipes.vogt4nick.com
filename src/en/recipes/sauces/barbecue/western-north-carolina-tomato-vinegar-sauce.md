# Western North Carolina tomato-vinegar sauce

=== "Ingredients"

    - 180 g
        [Black Peppercorn-Infused Apple Cider Vinegar](../../seasonings/infused-vinegar.md)
    - 120 g Ketchup or Tomato Sauce
    - 15 g Brown Sugar or Granulated Sugar
    - 15 g Worcestershire Sauce
    - 4 g Chili Powder
    - 2 g Kosher Salt
    - Hot Sauce

    !!! info "Yields about 250 g"

=== "Directions"

    1. **Combine** all ingredients in a small saucepan.
    2. **Simmer** gently 5-10 minutes, stirring until blended.
    3. **Cool.** Remove from heat and allow to cool to room temperature.
    4. **Season** to taste.

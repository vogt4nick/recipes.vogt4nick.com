# Red chili garlic chutney

=== "Ingredients"

    - 7 Dried Cayenne Pepper, stemmed
    - 7 Garlic Cloves
    - Salt
    - 80 g Water

!!! info "Yields about 240 g."

=== "Directions"

    1. **Soak chilies** 30 minutes in 160 g hot water until softened. Drain.
    2. **Blend** soaked chilies, garlic, salt, and water until smooth. Keep paste
        thick.

!!! info "Refrigerate up to 2 weeks."

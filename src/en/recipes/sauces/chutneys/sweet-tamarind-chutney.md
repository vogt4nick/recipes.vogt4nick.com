# Sweet tamarind chutney

=== "Ingredients"

    - 50 g Seedless Tamarind, packed
    - 100 g Seedless Dates
    - 95 g Jaggery, grated
    - 475 g Water
    - 1/2 t Cumin Seed, roasted, ground
    - 1/2 t Coriander Seed, ground
    - 1/2 t Dried Ginger Root, ground
    - 1/4 t Dried Cayenne Pepper, ground
    - Black Salt

    !!! info "Yields about 240 g."

    !!! tip "Dates can be omitted; double the jaggery. Dates give better body."

=== "Directions"

    1. **Cook tamarind and dates.** Combine tamarind, dates, and water in a
        saucepan. Cook on low heat 8-9 minutes until both soften.
    2. **Dissolve jaggery.** Add jaggery and stir until dissolved. Let mixture
        thicken slightly.
    3. **Season.** Add cumin, coriander, ginger, and chile powder. Stir and simmer
        1-2 minutes. Season with salt. Remove from heat. Cool completely.
    4. **Blend** until smooth. Add water if needed for desired consistency.
    5. **Strain** through a sieve to remove fibrous bits.

!!! info "Refrigerate up to 1 month."

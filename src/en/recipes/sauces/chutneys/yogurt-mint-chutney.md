# Yogurt mint chutney

=== "Ingredients"

    - 120 g Plain Yogurt, full-fat
    - Fresh Mint Leaves, packed
    - Fresh Coriander Leaves, packed
    - 1 Green Chile
    - 1/2 t Cumin Seed, ground
    - 1/4 t Garam Masala (optional)
    - 1/2 t Sugar
    - 1 t Lemon Juice
    - Salt
    - 30 g Water, chilled

    !!! info "Yields about 240 g"

    !!! tip "Strain regular yogurt through cheesecloth 30 minutes to thicken."

=== "Directions"

    1. **Clean herbs.** Remove thick stalks from mint, coriander, and chile. Tender
        stalks are fine.
    2. **Blend herb paste.** Place mint, coriander, chile, salt, water, and lemon
        juice in a blender. Blend until smooth.
    3. **Combine.** Transfer yogurt to a bowl. Add herb paste, cumin, garam masala,
        and sugar. Whisk until incorporated.
    4. **Chill** until serving.

    !!! tip "Do not add yogurt to the blender with the herbs. It will go too thin."

!!! info "Refrigerate up to 3 days."

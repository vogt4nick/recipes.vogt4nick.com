=== "Ingredients"

    - Phase 1
        - 12 Eggs, yolks only
        - 220 g Jamaican Rum (Appleton Estate, 43% ABV)
        - 220 g Cognac (Ferrand, 40% ABV)
        - 220 g Bourbon (Old Forester, 50% ABV)
        - 240 g Whole Milk
        - 450 g Sugar
        - 2/3 t Nutmeg, grated
    - Phase 2
        - 240 g Whole Milk
        - 480 g Half-and-Half
        - 470 g Heavy Cream
        - 1/3 t Nutmeg, grated

    !!! info "Yields 8-10 servings"

=== "Phase 1"

    1. **Separate eggs.** Put yolks in a 2 L container. Reserve whites for another
        use.
    2. **Combine spirits** in a jug with a pour spout.
    3. **Blend yolks** with phase 1 milk, sugar, and nutmeg using a stick blender
        until smooth. Keep blender head submerged to limit foam.
    4. **Add spirits.** With blender running, pour in spirits slowly. Blend until
        incorporated.
    5. **Chill** 30 days covered in refrigerator. Shake occasionally.

    !!! warning "Keep phase 1 at or above 20% ABV."

        Phase 1 relies on >20% ABV to reduce any salmonella population. Estimate phase 1
        ABV before substituting spirits:

        | Component              | Alcohol (g) | Alcohol (ml) |  Total (g) |  Total (ml) |
        | :--------------------- | ----------: | -----------: | ---------: | ----------: |
        | Jamaican Rum (43% ABV) |        79 g |       100 ml |      220 g |      233 ml |
        | Cognac (40% ABV)       |        73 g |        93 ml |      220 g |      232 ml |
        | Bourbon (50% ABV)      |        93 g |       118 ml |      220 g |      236 ml |
        | Whole Milk             |         0 g |         0 ml |      240 g |      232 ml |
        | Sugar                  |         0 g |         0 ml |      450 g |      284 ml |
        | Egg Yolks              |         0 g |         0 ml |      204 g |      198 ml |
        | **Total**              |   **245 g** |   **311 ml** | **1554 g** | **1415 ml** |

        311 ml ÷ 1415 ml ≈ 22.0% ABV.

=== "Phase 2"

    1. **Add dairy.** Move to a container of at least 3 L. With blender running, add
        phase 2 milk, half-and-half, cream, and nutmeg. Blend until smooth.
    2. **Chill** 50-60 days sealed in refrigerator. Shake occasionally.
    3. **Serve** cold.

    !!! tip "Aim to keep fat content between 10-11% by mass."

        | Component                       |   Fat (g) |  Total (g) |
        | :------------------------------ | --------: | ---------: |
        | Jamaican Rum (43% ABV)          |       0 g |      220 g |
        | Cognac (40% ABV)                |       0 g |      220 g |
        | Bourbon (50% ABV)               |       0 g |      220 g |
        | Whole Milk, phase 1 (3.25% fat) |       8 g |      240 g |
        | Sugar                           |       0 g |      450 g |
        | Egg Yolks (26.5% fat)           |      54 g |      204 g |
        | Whole Milk, phase 2 (3.25% fat) |       8 g |      240 g |
        | Half-and-Half (11.5% fat)       |      55 g |      480 g |
        | Heavy Cream (36% fat)           |     169 g |      470 g |
        | **Total**                       | **294 g** | **2744 g** |

        294 g ÷ 2744 g ≈ 10.7% fat by mass.

!!! info "Store up to 6 months chilled and unopened."

=== "Ingredients"

    - 2 slices White Bread
    - 10 g [Tartar Sauce](../sauces/hollandaise/mayonnaise/tartar-sauce.md)
    - Fish Sticks
    - Ketchup
    - Hot Sauce

=== "Directions"

    1. **Cook fish sticks** per package directions.
    2. **Dress sandwich.** Spread tartar sauce on bottom slice. Add fish sticks.
        Spread ketchup or hot sauce on top slice. Close sandwich.

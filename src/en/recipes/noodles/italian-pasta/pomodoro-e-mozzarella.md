=== "Ingredients"

    - 2 T Olive Oil
    - 1 Garlic Clove
    - 400 g Whole Peeled Tomatoes
    - Salt
    - Fresh Basil Leaves
    - 250 g Mozzarella, chopped
    - 320 g Dry Fusilli
    - 60 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
    - Black Peppercorn, ground

=== "Directions"

    1. **Infuse oil.** Warm oil and whole garlic in a saucepan. Add tomatoes. Season
        with salt.
    2. **Simmer sauce.** Add basil. Simmer over low heat 15-20 minutes until
        tomatoes reduce. Discard garlic.
    3. **Drain mozzarella** in a colander to remove excess whey.
    4. **Cook pasta** in boiling salted water per package directions until al dente.
        Reserve 1 ladle of cooking water. Drain directly into pan with sauce.
    5. **Toss.** Stir well. Remove from heat. Add parmigiano and mozzarella.
    6. **Finish.** Drizzle with olive oil. Add pepper. Stir quickly until combined
        but mozzarella has not melted. Serve immediately.

    !!! tip "For a creamier sauce, add cooking water while tossing."

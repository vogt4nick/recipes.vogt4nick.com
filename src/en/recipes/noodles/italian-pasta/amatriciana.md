=== "Ingredients"

    - 320 g Dry Spaghetti
    - 400 g Whole Peeled Tomatoes
    - 150 g Guanciale, rind removed, chopped into ~5 mm thick strips
    - 50 g White Wine
    - 1 Fresh Red Chili, seeded
    - 75 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
    - Salt

=== "Directions"

    1. **Boil water.**
    2. **Render guanciale.** Cook guanciale in a cast iron or steel pan over low
        heat 7-8 minutes, stirring often, until fat is translucent and edges are
        crisp. Do not add oil.
    3. **Deglaze.** Add wine. Raise heat to high until wine evaporates.
    4. **Reserve guanciale.** Lift guanciale out into a plate, leaving fat in the
        pan.
    5. **Simmer sauce.** Add chili and tomatoes to pan. Simmer over medium-low heat
        10 minutes. Season with salt.
    6. **Cook pasta** in boiling water per package directions until al dente.
        Reserve some cooking water. Drain.
    7. **Combine.** Remove chili from sauce. Stir in guanciale. Add pasta and toss
        quickly to coat. For softer pasta, add a splash of cooking water and cook a
        little longer.
    8. **Finish.** Sprinkle with pecorino. Serve immediately.

!!! warning "Do not substitute pancetta for guanciale."

    Guanciale is two parts fat to one part meat and tastes almost sweet, which pairs
    with the saltiness of Pecorino Romano. Pancetta is not as delicate.

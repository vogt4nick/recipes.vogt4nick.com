=== "Ingredients"

    - 320 g Dry Spaghetti
    - 150 g Guanciale, rind removed, chopped into ~1 cm thick strips
    - 6 Eggs, yolks only
    - 50 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
    - Black Peppercorn, ground
    - Salt

    !!! info "Yields 4 servings"

=== "Directions"

    1. **Boil salt water.**
    2. **Render guanciale.** Cook guanciale in a cold, dry pan over medium heat
        10-12 minutes until fat is rendered and edges are crisp. Do not add oil.
        Remove from heat.
    3. **Make sauce.** Whisk yolks, pecorino, and pepper in a bowl until smooth.
    4. **Cook pasta** in boiling salted water per package directions until al dente.
        Reserve 1 cup cooking water. Drain.
    5. **Combine.** Add pasta to guanciale and toss to coat in fat. Remove from heat
        and let cool 1 minute.
    6. **Add sauce.** Pour in yolk mixture. Stir vigorously, adding cooking water by
        the spoonful until sauce is creamy and coats pasta.

    !!! warning "Do not add yolks over direct heat."

        Yolks scramble above about 70ºC (158ºF).

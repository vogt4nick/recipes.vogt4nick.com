=== "Ingredients"

    - 320 g Dry Spaghetti or Rigatoni
    - 240 g Guanciale, chopped into ~1 cm thick strips
    - 60 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
    - Black Peppercorn, ground
    - Salt

=== "Directions"

    1. **Boil salt water.**
    2. **Cook pasta** in boiling water per package directions until al dente.
        Reserve 1 cup cooking water. Drain.
    3. **Render guanciale.** Cook guanciale in a cold, dry pan over medium heat
        10-12 minutes until fat is rendered and edges are crisp. Do not add oil.
    4. **Finish pasta.** Add pasta and 1/4 cup cooking water to guanciale. Stir over
        medium heat 1 minute until water is absorbed.
    5. **Add cheese.** Remove from heat. Add pecorino and pepper. Stir vigorously,
        adding cooking water by the spoonful until sauce coats pasta.

    !!! warning "Do not add cheese over direct heat."

        Pecorino turns stringy and clumps when overheated.

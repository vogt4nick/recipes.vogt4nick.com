=== "Ingredients"

    - 250 g Dry Bucatini or Spaghetti
    - 50 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
    - 25 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
    - 2 t Black Peppercorn, coarsely crushed
    - Salt

=== "Directions"

    1. **Boil salt water.**
    2. **Cook pasta** 3 minutes less than package directions until just under al
        dente.
    3. **Make paste.** Mix cheeses with a pinch of pepper. Stir in cooking water a
        spoonful at a time until the texture is between ricotta and mascarpone.
    4. **Toast pepper.** Toast remaining pepper in a dry pan 20-30 seconds, moving
        constantly. Add a ladle of cooking water and simmer until slightly
        thickened.
    5. **Combine.** Add pasta to pan and toss to coat, adding cooking water until
        very saucy. Remove from heat and let cool 1 minute.
    6. **Add cheese.** Add paste all at once. Toss vigorously until sauce is creamy,
        adding cooking water if it turns grainy.
    7. **Serve** topped with more pecorino and pepper.
